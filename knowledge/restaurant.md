## Dietary information

- Vegetarian options: hummus, moutabal, tabbouleh, fattoush, warak enab, labneh, cheese sambousek, spinach fatayer, batata harra, grilled halloumi, and most desserts.
- Vegan options: hummus, moutabal, tabbouleh, fattoush, warak enab, spinach fatayer, batata harra, fruit platter.
- Gluten-free: dishes marked (GF). Grills are gluten-free without the bread, except arayes kafta, which is grilled inside bread. Fattoush, tabbouleh, kibbeh, sambousek and fatayer contain gluten.
- Nuts: dishes marked (N) contain pine nuts or pistachios.
- The kitchen handles gluten, nuts, dairy and sesame. Cross-contact cannot be ruled out. Customers with a severe allergy should speak with the team before ordering.

## Bookings

- Bookings can be made through this assistant, by phone, or on Instagram.
- To book, the assistant needs: name, phone number, date, time, number of people, and seating preference (indoor or terrace). Special requests are optional.
- Online bookings are for up to 10 people. For 11 people or more, or for private events, the team calls the customer back to arrange it.
- Tables are held for 15 minutes after the booking time.
- Bookings can be made up to 30 days in advance.
- To change or cancel a booking, the customer messages the assistant or calls the restaurant.
- The terrace is covered and heated in winter.

## Payment

- Accepted: cash in USD or LBP, Visa and Mastercard, and Whish.
- Service is not included in the bill.

## Other information

- Parking: valet parking is available every evening from 18:00 for $3.
- Birthdays: customers may bring their own cake. A cake fee of $1 per person applies. The team can add candles and a birthday song on request.
- Kids: high chairs are available. There is no separate kids menu, but the kitchen can prepare a small plate of taouk with fries for $6.
- Wifi: free for customers.
- Argileh (shisha): not available.
- Takeaway: available. Order through the assistant or by phone and pick up at the restaurant.
- Catering for events: available for 20 people or more. The team calls the customer back with a quote.

## When the assistant must hand over to the team

The assistant connects the customer with the team (by saying the team will call or message them back, and saving their name and phone number) when:

- the customer has a complaint, a problem with an order, or wants a refund
- the customer has a severe allergy
- the booking is for 11 people or more, a private event, or catering
- the customer asks something not covered in this information
- the customer asks to talk to a person
